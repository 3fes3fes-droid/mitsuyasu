Google is doing some great AI work in India that is actually helping farmers on the ground and honestly, almost nobody is talking about it.

Google DeepMind’s AnthroKrishi team built two AI models using satellite imagery:

- Agricultural Landscape Understanding (ALU) maps farm boundaries, trees and water bodies, with historical data going back 15 years.
- Agricultural Monitoring & Event Detection (AMED) uses multispectral data to monitor crops, sowing and harvesting, identifying 11 major crops.

- 5M+ farmers in Telangana through its agricultural DPI
- 140M+ hectares covered by Terrastack
- 2.6M hectares of irrigated land in Karnataka
- CarbonFarm is using it across 12 countries
- FAO is integrating the models into its global agricultural data platform with $2.5M from Google

These models are now providing agricultural insights across 6 African countries, and the Agricultural Landscape Understanding layer is one of the most popular layers on Google Earth globally.
